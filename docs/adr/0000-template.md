# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-....md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What is true today that forces a decision? The constraint, the bug, the request.
Include the evidence — a measurement, an error, a limitation of a dependency — not
just an assertion. Someone reading this in a year should understand the pressure
without having been here.

## Decision

What we are doing, stated plainly and in the present tense: "The share view renders
its own page switcher rather than tldraw's."

## Alternatives considered

Each one, and the specific reason it lost. An alternative with no stated cost was
not really considered. This section is the reason ADRs are worth writing.

## Consequences

What this buys, and what it costs — including the parts we accept and dislike.
Note anything a future change must not break, and anything that becomes harder.

## Follow-ups

Anything deliberately left undone, so it is recorded rather than forgotten.
Omit the section if there is nothing.
